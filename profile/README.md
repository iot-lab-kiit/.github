## About Us

Established on **5th December 2016** under the **School of Computer Engineering, KIIT University**, the IoT Lab is a premier student research forum and **Centre of Excellence** dedicated to research, innovation, and skill development. Currently led by **Prof. Sankalp Nayak**, the lab brings together a diverse team of students, faculty, and researchers united by a shared goal: build useful technology and use it to make everyday life a little better.

We look for problems that matter, work through them until the solution feels useful and real, and rely on research, hands-on testing, and honest iteration to keep the work grounded.

> _One of the best parts of working here is that there is always something new to learn._

## Domains

IoT Lab operates through specialized guilds spanning both technical and non-technical domains:

### Technical

| Domain | Description |
|---|---|
| **Internet of Things (IoT)** | Bridging the physical and digital worlds through embedded systems, sensors, and smart automation. |
| **App Development** | Crafting cross-platform mobile experiences with Flutter, React Native, and native tooling. |
| **Web Development** | Building fast, responsive web apps with React, Next.js, and modern full-stack technologies. |
| **Machine Learning** | Exploring deep learning, NLP, and computer vision to build systems that learn and adapt. |
| **Cyber Security** | Defending systems through ethical hacking, penetration testing, and hands-on security research. |
| **Competitive Programming** | Sharpening algorithmic thinking through contests, data structures, and relentless problem-solving. |

### Non-Technical

| Domain | Description |
|---|---|
| **Content** | Telling the lab's story through technical blogs, social copy, and event communications. |
| **GD & UI/UX** | Designing intuitive interfaces and bold graphics through research-driven, human-centered design. |
| **Video** | Documenting events and crafting cinematic reels through visual storytelling. |
| **Marketing** | Driving outreach, sponsorships, and campaigns that amplify the lab's presence and impact. |
| **Administration** | Running operations, events, and logistics — the backbone that keeps the lab moving. |

## Services We Offer

- **App Development** — We build apps by understanding the problem first. The goal is practical tech that solves something real.
- **Web Solutions** — Clear, fast, and easy-to-use websites and web infrastructure (hosting, storage, maintenance).
- **Event Management** — End-to-end event planning and execution, from the first call to the wrap-up.
